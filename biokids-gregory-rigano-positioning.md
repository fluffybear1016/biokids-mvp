## Handling the HCQ Question

Someone will eventually bring it up. Here's how to handle it in different contexts:

### If asked directly in a crunchy mom group:

> "Yes, I published research on hydroxychloroquine during COVID. Elon Musk shared it. I went on Tucker Carlson. The entire mainstream media attacked me for it. That experience taught me something important: the establishment doesn't always tell you the truth about what's safe and what's not. That same skepticism is why I started looking into what's really in our kids' food — and what I found about glyphosate and growth hormone is just as alarming."

### If asked in a sports parent group:

> "I'm a pharmaceutical lawyer and yes, I did research during COVID that got a lot of attention. My main focus now is children's growth science — I built bioKids because my brother's kids all want to go pro and I wanted to understand the actual science of how kids grow."

### If asked in a professional/podcast context:

> "My background is in pharmaceutical and environmental law. During COVID, I published research that got significant attention — Elon Musk shared it, I appeared on national TV. That experience gave me deep exposure to how the intersection of science, industry, and public health actually works. It's the same lens I now bring to children's growth science. The food industry is suppressing information that parents need, and I've seen this playbook before."

### The golden rule: Never be defensive. Own it. Pivot to bioKids.

| Scaling past 1,000 subs requires broader audience beyond crunchy moms | High (but planned) | This is Phase 2. Once you have 1,000 passionate subscribers + testimonials + social proof, you can soften the positioning for mainstream parents. The first 1,000 are your army. |
