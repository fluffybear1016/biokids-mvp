# bioKids — Master Plan to 1,000 Paying Subscribers

## The Philosophy

You're one person. Every action has to compound. Every hour spent today should still be generating subscribers 6 months from now. That means: content over ads, organic over paid, evergreen over ephemeral, and systems that build on each other.

**bioKids is a faceless brand.** No personality-driven content. No face on camera. The brand is the product — clinical, well-cited, trustworthy science content. Gregory Rigano exists behind the scenes for B2B credibility (cold emails, podcast pitches, influencer deals) but the public sees "bioKids," not a person. This is the Examine.com / Healthline model, not the Huberman model.

**All articles are free.** The research is the marketing. The paid product is the app — Growth Stacks, the AI assistant (Au), the Growth Windows, the tracking system, the height potential calculator, the age-personalized protocols. Free articles build trust and SEO. The app converts trust into revenue.

**No filming.** All content is 100% digital — Canva fact cards, CapCut kinetic typography videos, HeyGen AI avatar explainers. One person with a laptop can produce 20-30 pieces of content per week without touching a camera.

**Each article is an event.** One article per week, with a coordinated 5-day release across all channels. Not a dump — a launch.

The plan is sequential. Each step creates the foundation the next step needs. Don't skip ahead.

There are really only **3 automated channels** that matter:

1. **Automated cold email** pointed at 5 audiences (Cody Schneider playbook)
2. **SEO + GEO** (already built, compounds automatically)
3. **Digital content engine** (Canva + CapCut + HeyGen → 5 platforms via Repurpose.io, zero filming)

Everything else (Instagram backdoor, Facebook groups, Quora, Medium, X threads) is manual amplification layered on top.

## The Content Production Pipeline (Zero Filming)

All content is digital. You never touch a camera.

### Tier 1: Fact Cards (highest volume — 10-15/week)
- **Tool:** Canva Pro with a branded template
- **Format:** Bold stat + citation + bioKids watermark on a dark/clean background
- **Example:** "Eating before bed suppresses growth hormone by up to 80%. — Hartman et al., JCEM 1992"
- **Platforms:** Instagram feed, Pinterest, X, Facebook
- **Time per card:** 3-5 minutes once the template is set up
- **Batch:** Design 15-20 in one sitting (1 hour), schedule for 2 weeks

### Tier 2: Kinetic Typography Videos (medium volume — 5-7/week)
- **Tool:** CapCut (free) or Canva Video
- **Format:** Text appearing word by word over dark background with dramatic music. 15-45 seconds. The "clinical science fact" format that gets millions of views.
- **Example:** "Your kid's oatmeal..." [pause] "...contains a chemical..." [pause] "...that suppresses growth hormone..." [pause] "...by up to 80%." [citation slides in] "Hartman et al., Journal of Clinical Endocrinology, 1992." [bioKids logo]
- **Platforms:** TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, Pinterest Video Pins, Facebook (via Repurpose.io — upload once, posts to all 5)
- **Time per video:** 15-20 minutes in CapCut
- **Batch:** Make 7-10 in one sitting (2-3 hours), schedule across 2 weeks
- **Hook variation:** For each video, create 2-3 versions with different opening text. Post all versions. Let the algorithm pick the winner.

### Tier 3: HeyGen Avatar Explainers (lower volume — 2-3/week)
- **Tool:** HeyGen ($29/mo)
- **Format:** Stock avatar "talking head" explaining a concept. 45-90 seconds. Looks like UGC but no face required.
- **Pick an avatar:** Choose a clean, trustworthy-looking person. Or a female avatar to match the mom audience. This is NOT you — it's a brand spokesperson.
- **Example:** Avatar explains the sprint-GH connection, the sleep architecture concept, or the oatmeal problem in plain language.
- **Platforms:** Same 5 platforms via Repurpose.io
- **Time per video:** Write script (10 min) → paste into HeyGen → generate (5 min) → export
- **Batch:** Write 5 scripts (1 hour), generate all 5 in HeyGen (30 min), schedule

### Weekly Content Output:
- 10-15 fact cards (1 hour batch)
- 5-7 kinetic typography videos (2-3 hours batch)
- 2-3 HeyGen avatar videos (1.5 hours batch)
- **Total: 17-25 pieces of content per week, ~5 hours of work, ZERO filming**

## What NOT to Do

1. **Don't film anything.** Canva + CapCut + HeyGen produce better, more consistent content than shaky phone videos. And you can produce 10x the volume.
2. **Don't put your face on the brand.** bioKids is the brand. Greg is behind the scenes.
3. **Don't gate the articles.** Free articles are your marketing engine. The app is the product.
4. **Don't run all 7 cold email campaigns at once.** You'll drown in replies.
5. **Don't spend money on ads before organic proves the messaging.**
6. **Don't post in Facebook groups before lurking for 2 weeks.**
7. **Don't approach paid influencers before your social accounts have 30+ posts.**
8. **Don't dump all articles at once.** Each one is an event with a 5-day coordinated release.
9. **Don't manually outreach 68 verticals.** One automated cold email system, 5 audiences.
10. **Don't use "pharmaceutical lawyer."** Use "environmental lawyer" everywhere.
