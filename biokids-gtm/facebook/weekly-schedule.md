# Facebook Group Weekly Schedule
# Total time: ~30 min/day (the ONLY thing you can't automate — human vibrations required)

---

## DAILY ROUTINE (every day, 15-20 min)

1. Open Facebook → check notifications from your groups
2. Respond to any comments on your previous posts/comments
3. Scroll 3-4 groups → find 3-5 posts to comment on
4. Use comment templates from comment-templates.md
5. Like 10-15 posts (shows you're active, not just selling)

## WEEKLY POSTING SCHEDULE

### Monday — Comment Day (no posts, just engage)
- Comment on 5+ posts across Tier 1 groups
- Focus: Earthly Mamas + Travel Baseball Families
- Goal: be seen as helpful, build recognition

### Tuesday — Comment Day
- Comment on 5+ posts across Tier 1 groups
- Focus: Crunchy Moms + Youth Basketball Dev
- Goal: answer questions, drop knowledge bombs (no links)

### Wednesday — VALUE POST in ONE Track 1 group
- Post a standalone insight (not the origin story, just a fact)
- Example: "Did you know that the timing of dinner relative to bedtime affects your child's growth hormone release? Eating high-glycemic food within 2 hours of sleep suppresses the GH pulse that happens in the first 90 minutes of deep sleep. Published in the Journal of Clinical Endocrinology & Metabolism."
- No links. Just value. Let people come to you.

### Thursday — Comment Day
- Comment on 5+ posts
- Focus: any group where your Wednesday post got traction → follow up in comments
- If someone asked for your research → share biokids.me link in the reply

### Friday — VALUE POST in ONE Track 2 group
- Post a sports-relevant insight
- Example: "Fun fact for sports parents: sprint-trained athletes produce nearly 3x the peak growth hormone of endurance-trained athletes. A single 30-second all-out sprint raises GH by 700%. The research on why explosive training builds better athletes is fascinating."
- No links unless someone asks.

### Saturday & Sunday — Light engagement
- Like a few posts, maybe 1-2 comments
- Weekend posts get less traction anyway
- Save your energy for Monday

## MONTHLY MILESTONES

### Week 1-2: Foundation (join groups, lurk, comment only)
- Join Tier 1 groups (9 groups)
- Read 20-30 posts per group to understand culture
- Start commenting with knowledge bombs
- DO NOT post, DO NOT share links yet

### Week 3: First Origin Story
- Post Track 1 origin story in Earthly Mamas (356K) — see origin-story-track1.md
- Wait for engagement, respond to every comment personally
- When people ask for the link → share in comments

### Week 4: Second Origin Story
- Post Track 2 origin story in Travel Baseball Families (95K) — see origin-story-track2.md
- Post Track 1 origin story in Crunchy Moms (288K)
- Continue daily commenting routine

### Week 5+: Established Presence
- You're now "the pharma lawyer who researches kids' growth"
- Start sharing biokids.me links naturally in comments
- Post 1-2 value posts per week with links where appropriate
- Join Tier 2 groups, repeat the cycle

## ANTI-BAN CHECKLIST (review before every post)

- [ ] Am I posting the same link in multiple groups today? → DON'T
- [ ] Is this my first post in this group? → Comment first for 2 weeks
- [ ] Have I engaged 5x more than I've posted? → If not, comment more
- [ ] Does this group have self-promo rules? → Check pinned post
- [ ] Am I posting as Gregory Rigano (not Don James)? → Must be Greg
