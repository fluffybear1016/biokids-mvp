# Daily Voice Commands — Your Entire Day in SuperWhisper
# Total time: ~45 min of speaking + 15 min of human responses = 1 hour/day

---

## MORNING (15 min speaking, while drinking coffee)

### Command 1: "Pull today's numbers"
What the agent does:
- Checks Instantly for: emails sent, opens, replies, bounces across all campaigns
- Checks GA4 for: site visitors, page views, sign-ups in last 24 hours
- Checks Facebook Ads (if running): spend, clicks, CPC, conversions
- Formats a summary and sends it to Slack

### Command 2: "Any hot replies?"
What the agent does:
- Checks Instantly for all new replies in last 24 hours
- Classifies each: interested, question, not interested, out of office
- Sends you a Slack message with ONLY the interested + question replies
- Auto-archives "not interested" and reschedules "out of office"

### Command 3: "Generate today's content from [article URL]"
What the agent does:
- Reads the bioKids article
- Creates: 1 X/Twitter thread (10 tweets), 1 Medium draft, 3 TikTok scripts (30s each), 1 Quora answer, 5 Instagram carousel slide texts
- Saves everything to content/ folder
- You review and post the ones you like (5 min)

---

## MIDDAY (15 min human engagement — THIS IS WHERE YOU BRING THE VIBES)

### Facebook Groups (15-20 min, can't automate this — it's your superpower)
1. Open Facebook
2. Check your 3-4 most active groups
3. Find 3-5 posts to comment on → use comment-templates.md
4. Respond to any replies on your previous comments/posts
5. Like 10-15 posts (takes 2 min, shows you're active)

### Post Generated Content (5 min)
- Review what Agent generated this morning in content/ folder
- Copy-paste the X/Twitter thread → post from @RiganoESQ
- Copy-paste the Medium draft → publish on Medium
- Save TikTok scripts for when you record (batch 1x/week)

---

## EVENING (5 min)

### Command 4: "Anything new since this morning?"
What the agent does:
- Checks for new replies since morning check
- Checks for any new podcast booking requests (Cal.com)
- Sends quick Slack summary

### If there are interested replies that need personal response:
- YOU write the response (human vibrations, not AI)
- These are warm leads who replied to your cold email — they deserve real Greg

---

## WEEKLY (run on Monday morning, 30 min total)

### Command 5: "Weekly report"
What the agent does:
- Pulls 7-day metrics from Instantly, GA4, Facebook Ads
- Compares to last week: what's up, what's down
- Identifies top-performing campaign, worst-performing campaign
- Counts total new subscribers this week
- Sends comprehensive Slack report + saves to data/weekly-reports/

### Command 6: "Scrape new leads for [campaign name]"
What the agent does:
- Searches Raphonic/Apollo/PhantomBuster for new leads matching campaign criteria
- Deduplicates against existing lead lists in leads/ folder
- Validates through Million Verifier
- Loads into Instantly
- Updates lead count in Slack

### Command 7 (if LinkedIn post got engagement): "Scrape LinkedIn engagers from [post URL]"
What the agent does:
- PhantomBuster scrapes all likers + commenters
- Apollo enriches with emails
- Million Verifier validates
- Loads into Instantly Campaign 7 (LinkedIn warm leads)
