# Facebook Ad Creative Generator — Voice Command Script
# Budget: $5-10/day for 30 days ($150-300 total)
# Goal: Test 30-50 creative variants, find the 3-5 winners

## STEP 1: Generate Creatives (Day 14)

Voice command:
"Generate 30 Facebook ad variations for bioKids. Create them as React components with 1080x1080 dimensions. Use the bioKids color scheme (earth tones, clean, science-meets-parenting). Create 6 variations for each of these 5 angles:

Angle 1 — PHARMA LAWYER HOOK
Headlines:
- 'A pharma lawyer looked at your kids' oatmeal'
- 'What a pharmaceutical lawyer found in children's cereal'
- 'I spent years in pharma law. Then I looked at my nephew's breakfast.'
Body: Glyphosate in conventional oats suppresses growth hormone in children. This is in JAMA. Nobody is talking about it.

Angle 2 — GROWTH BLOCKERS LIST
Headlines:
- '18 things secretly stunting your child's growth'
- 'You're probably doing 12 of these 18 things'
- 'A pharma lawyer found 18 growth blockers in most homes'
Body: Free research from JAMA, Endocrine Reviews — what's suppressing your child's growth hormone.

Angle 3 — SPRINTING SCIENCE
Headlines:
- 'A 30-second sprint increases growth hormone by 700%'
- 'The #1 exercise for your child's growth (it takes 30 seconds)'
- 'Why sprint-trained kids produce 3x more growth hormone'
Body: Peer-reviewed science on the most powerful natural growth trigger.

Angle 4 — DENNIS RODMAN
Headlines:
- 'Dennis Rodman was 5'8" after high school'
- 'How Dennis Rodman grew 12 inches after age 19'
- 'The biology behind late growth spurts is real'
Body: The science of delayed epiphyseal fusion — and what it means for your child.

Angle 5 — BIG FOOD CONSPIRACY
Headlines:
- 'What Big Food doesn't want parents to know'
- 'The food industry is suppressing your child's growth'
- 'A pharma lawyer exposes what's in your kids' food supply'
Body: Peer-reviewed research the food industry doesn't want you to see.

For each variation, include:
- Headline text (large, bold)
- 2-line body text
- biokids.me link
- 'Free Research' or 'Read the Science' CTA button
- Clean design, white background with accent color

Export each as a 1080x1080 PNG to the creative/ folder. Name them: angle1-v1.png through angle5-v6.png"

## STEP 2: Upload to Facebook Ads (Day 14)

Voice command:
"Take all 30 ad creatives from creative/ folder. Upload them to Facebook Ads using the API. Create campaign 'bioKids — Creative Test'. Create 5 ad sets (one per angle). Put 6 creatives in each ad set. Set each ad set budget to $2/day. Target: Women 28-55, United States, interests in 'organic food', 'natural parenting', 'holistic health', 'non-toxic living', 'clean eating'. Set all to PAUSED so I can review."

## STEP 3: Review + Launch (Day 14-15)

- Greg reviews the 30 ad previews in Facebook Ads Manager
- Approves or rejects each one
- Launches approved ads

## STEP 4: Analyze Performance (Day 21, Day 28, Day 35, Day 44)

Voice command (run weekly):
"Pull Facebook ad performance data for the bioKids campaign from the last 7 days. Show me: which ad sets have the lowest cost per click? Which creatives have the highest CTR? Which ones have the best conversion rate? Pause any ad that has spent more than $15 with zero link clicks. Send me a Slack summary."

## STEP 5: Scale Winners or Kill (Day 44)

If 2-3 ads are clearly winning (CTR >2%, CPC <$0.75):
"Create a new ad set called 'Winners - Scale'. Add the top 3 performing ads. Set budget to $10/day. Keep running for another 30 days."

If nothing is winning:
"Pause the entire campaign. We'll stick with organic."
