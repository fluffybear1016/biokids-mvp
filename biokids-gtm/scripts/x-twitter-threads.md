## THREAD 2: Dennis Rodman Growth Story (Week 2)

1/ Dennis Rodman was 5'8" after high school.

He grew to 6'8".

The biology behind this is one of the most fascinating things I've ever researched. 🧵

2/ Rodman experienced what's called delayed epiphyseal fusion.

Your growth plates (epiphyses) are soft cartilage at the ends of your bones. They harden and close when estrogen signals tell them to.

Rodman's plates stayed open far longer than normal.

3/ Most males' growth plates close between 16-20.

Rodman's didn't close until his early 20s. His body kept producing growth hormone, and with open plates, that hormone had somewhere to go.

12 inches of growth. After high school.

4/ This isn't a miracle. It's biology.

Delayed puberty = later growth plate closure = longer growth window.

And it's more common than you think. About 2-3% of adolescents experience constitutional delay of growth and puberty (CDGP).

5/ Here's what this means for parents: the growth window is real, and it's influenced by factors you can actually control.

Peak growth hormone is released in the first 90 minutes of deep sleep. Sprint training triggers a 700%+ GH spike.

And certain things close that window faster.

6/ Early puberty shortens the growth window.

Endocrine disruptors — chemicals in plastics, pesticides, processed foods — can trigger earlier puberty.

Earlier puberty = earlier plate closure = shorter adult height.

This is published in Endocrine Reviews.
